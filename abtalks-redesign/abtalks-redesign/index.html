THE LOOP

Three steps, repeated for 60 days

It's the same loop every day, so it becomes a habit instead of a decision you make each morning.

STEP_01

Get today's task

Open your dashboard and get one scoped task for your track — AI, Data Science, or Software Engineering. No vague "learn X," a real thing to build.

STEP_02

Build it, then prove it

Push your commit to GitHub and post about it on LinkedIn. Both links go on your submission — that's your proof of work, publicly checkable.

STEP_03

Watch your streak grow

Your dashboard tracks the streak, your standing, and your progress through all 60 days — visible to you, and eventually to recruiters.
